# PreySense v3.1.4

- **Added** built-in support for the **Acer Performance** menu in the modified RubyLight preview, with live telemetry, performance modes, and Auto, Custom, or Max fans.
- **Added** desktop fan-speed sliders, live fan-editor readings, and a **Skip this version** option for update prompts.
- **Improved** game-profile controls, keyboard lighting editing, and CPU power-limit readback.
- **Fixed** GPU power limits when switching quickly from Performance to Turbo, and made streaming Custom fan mode follow the saved curve.
- **Fixed** keyboard backlight idle timeout on the PHN16-73 and Predator special-key handling on supported Chicony keyboards.
- **Fixed** LCD Overdrive settings being lost after restart or resume.
- **Removed** the in-app Acer Services toggle and retired fan-delay settings. Acer service management is available through the bundled scripts.
